# On your phones

Huddle is a web app you install from the browser: no app store, no account.

## iPhone

In **Safari**, open the address, then *Share › Add to Home Screen*. Open the app from its new icon:
notifications only work from the installed app (iOS 16.4 or later).

## Android

In **Chrome**, open the address, then *⋮ › Install app* (or *Add to Home screen*).

## Notifications

In the app, go to **Profile** and turn notifications on. You'll get:

- a reminder an hour before a game you haven't picked;
- the results once a week is over;
- a heads-up when a pick of yours flips late in a game;
- the opening of the end-of-season bonus picks, and a reminder before they close.

If notifications were blocked once, allow them for the site in the phone's settings, then come back
to the profile.

## Logging in

Each player taps their name and types their 4-digit PIN. The first PIN typed becomes the player's
PIN; the admin can clear it if someone forgets theirs. Phones stay logged in for a year.
